General Spatial Model Specification

The report compares an OLS baseline with a spatial-error model for the same outcome and predictors. The baseline model is:

y = X beta + epsilon

The spatial-error model is:

y = X beta + u, where u = lambda W u + epsilon

  • y is the turnout outcome for each Census Tract.
  • X is the predictor matrix for the relevant block or combined model.
  • W is the row-standardized Census Tract neighbour spatial-weights matrix.
  • lambda measures spatial dependence in the unexplained/error component. A positive significant lambda means neighbouring CTs share residual turnout patterns after the model covariates are included.

Key metrics: Delta AIC is spatial-error AIC minus OLS AIC, so negative values favor the spatial-error model. Filtered Moran I checks whether spatial clustering remains after the spatial-error correction. Spatial effect = yes means the spatial-error term is statistically/substantively meaningful and the spatial model materially improves fit.
